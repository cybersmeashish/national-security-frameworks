# National Security Frameworks: cyber capability and evidence

Original research on a national cyber capability framework connecting government accountability, sovereign infrastructure, critical services, AI assurance, cryptographic dependencies, and recovery.

**Stage:** reference framework. This is an independent research workspace, with no claimed government appointment, classified material, official policy status, or national accreditation.

## Capability model

| Capability | Ownership question | Proposed evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Governance and accountability | Who may accept, delegate or review each security decision? | Mandate, decision authority, exception and review records |
| Critical-service dependencies | Which service or supplier failure can disrupt essential operations? | Scoped service map and dependency scenarios |
| Sovereign infrastructure | Who controls data, operators, keys, runtime and recovery? | Boundary register and locally observed controls |
| Identity and supply-chain assurance | Which identities, artifacts and changes are trusted? | Privilege, provenance, promotion and revocation cases |
| AI and application assurance | Which inputs or tools can affect an important action? | Threat model and allowed/denied regression evidence |
| Cryptographic transition | Which dependency, purpose or supplier constrains migration? | Inventory, ownership and compatibility evidence |
| Detection, response and recovery | Who can act when connectivity or services fail? | Authorized exercises, reporting and restoration records |
| Skills and independent evaluation | Who can reproduce and challenge the security claim? | Training, review, test and adoption records |

## Primary-source baselines

[NIST CSF 2.0](https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.CSWP.29) and [NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5](https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.SP.800-53r5) provide published outcomes and control material to examine. The capability grouping and record format here are my own design. A jurisdiction's mandates and applicability require their own authoritative review.
